Superstore Sales Performance Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyses the Sample Superstore Sales Dataset to evaluate retail business performance, identify sales trends, understand customer purchasing behaviour and assess profitability.

The project was completed as part of the AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship using Microsoft Power BI.

Project Objectives

 Analyse overall sales and profitability.
 Evaluate sales performance across different regions.
 Identify top-performing product categories.
 Examine customer segment contributions.
 Analyse monthly sales trends.
 Identify potential business risks and growth opportunities.
 Provide data-driven recommendations to support management decisions.

Tools Used

 Microsoft Excel – Data inspection and preparation.
 Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation.
 Microsoft Power BI – Data modelling, DAX calculations and dashboard development.

Key Performance Indicators

KPI| Result
Total Sales| $2,297,200.86
Total Profit| $286,397.02
Total Orders| 5,009
Average Sales| $229.86
Profit Margin| 12.47%

Key Business Insights

1. The West region recorded the highest sales.
2. Technology recorded the highest sales among product categories.
3. The Consumer segment contributed the highest sales.
4. Technology recorded the highest profit among product categories.
5. November recorded the highest monthly sales.

Business Recommendations

 Review loss-making transactions and products.
 Improve discount and pricing strategies.
 Strengthen marketing for high-performing product categories.
 Develop targeted strategies for different customer segments.
 Improve inventory planning ahead of peak sales periods.
 Monitor business performance regularly using the dashboard.

Project Deliverables

- Interactive Power BI dashboard (".pbix").
- Dashboard export.
- Business Intelligence Overview Report.
- Executive Summary Report.

Dataset

Source: "Sample Superstore Sales Dataset – Kaggle" (https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final)

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Business Intelligence and data visualisation can transform retail data into meaningful insights. The dashboard provides management with an overview of business performance and supports evidence-based decision-making.
